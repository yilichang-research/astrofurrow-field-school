Follow one tested Tuku decision

In the 72-hour lab, the 10 mm practice plan ends day three at 50.3% of plant-available soil water with the archived rain. Setting rain to zero in the same simulation brings the result to 48.9%, below the example 50% line.

The input is NASA POWER Daily API v2.10.3, IMERG_PRECTOT in mm/day, for 11–13 August 2024 UTC. These are regional grid estimates. The v0.8 lab adds that rain to the soil water balance with the same crop, soil settings, and pumping in both runs.

A local-only comparison is pending. The separate Tuku gauge example uses Taiwan civil days. Its strict quality screen leaves zero complete matched days. Before watering on a farm, check both soil layers and how the field drains.
